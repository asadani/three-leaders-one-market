Boston Consulting Group, Where's the Value in AI?: the 10, 20, 70 split of realized AI value (10% algorithms, 20% technology and data, 70% people and process). bcg.com

S&P Global Market Intelligence, enterprise AI survey: 42% of companies abandoned most of their AI initiatives, up from 17% the prior year. spglobal.com

Analyst surveys on agentic-AI adoption (2023, 2025) and the Gartner AI Hype Cycle, the year-over-year shift in what enterprises pursued (chatbots, then retrieval, then agents, then defensibility), evidence that the market's definition of success kept relocating. gartner.com
